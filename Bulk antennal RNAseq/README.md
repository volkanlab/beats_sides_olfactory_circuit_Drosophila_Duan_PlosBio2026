The files in this folder are related to the results presented in Figs S3A, S3D, and S11F. 
The analyses were based on the previously published RNAseq data.
The references are: 
Developmental WT antennae: Barish S, Nuss S, Strunilin I, Bao S, Mukherjee S, Jones CD, et al. Combinations of DIPs and Dprs control organization of olfactory receptor neuron terminals in Drosophila. PLoS Genet. 2018;14(8):e1007560.
Atonal mutant: Menuz K, Larter NK, Park J, Carlson JR. An RNA-seq screen of the Drosophila antenna identifies a transporter necessary for ammonia detection. PLoS Genet. 2014;10(11):e1004810.
Amos mutant: Mohapatra P, Menuz K. Molecular Profiling of the Drosophila Antenna Reveals Conserved Genes Underlying Olfaction in Insects. G3 (Bethesda). 2019;9(11):3753-71.
OR mutant: Deanhardt B, Duan Q, Du C, Soeder C, Morlote A, Garg D, et al. Social experience and pheromone receptor activity reprogram gene expression in sensory neurons. G3 (Bethesda). 2023;13(6).
