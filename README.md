# beats_sides_olfactory_circuit_Drosophila_Duan_PlosBio2026

R scripts for the analyses presented in: 

Duan Q, Okuwa S, Estrella R, Yeung C, Chen YD, Rio LQ, Du C, Vien KM, Volkan PC. Beat- and Side-family cell-surface molecules are expressed combinatorially in the partner neurons of the olfactory circuit in Drosophila. PLoS Biol. 2026 Aug 27;24(8):e3003955. doi: 10.1371/journal.pbio.3003955. PMID: 42659622; PMCID: PMC13521578.

The BioRxiv preprint was posted in 2025: Duan et al. (2025) Deciphering the Combinatorial Expression and Genetic Regulatory Mechanisms of Beats and Sides in the Olfactory Circuits of Drosophila. https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.05.31.657193
